grid.createGridTwoPhaseFlipSolver2 (packages/fluxflow/src/grid/grid_two_phase_flip_solver2.js) — a two-phase (liquid + gas) FLIP solver. Unlike grid.createGridFlipSolver2, the air here is a simulated phase rather than a Dirichlet p = 0 void, so it can push back. Each particle carries a phase tag (Boyd & Bridson's MultiFLIP, ACM TOG 2012); the phases are coupled through a variable-density pressure projection (Kang/Fedkiw/Liu 2000, Hong & Kim 2005), which is new faceWeights support in multigrid.js and grid_pressure_solver2.js. Nothing in this scene applies a buoyancy force. Gravity is uniform on every face, for both phases; the bubble rises purely because the projection knows the gas is lighter. The density-ratio slider changes how much lighter — drag it to 2 and the bubble barely moves. Expect the bubble to rise, deform into a cap, and break the surface; the water sloshes and settles. Open the console for a periodic diagnostic readout.

The classic "flow past a cylinder" benchmark: a fixed inflow at the left wall, an outflow at the right wall, a uniform force pushing the whole domain rightward, and a circular collider centered in the domain (diameter = 1/3 of the domain's side length). Dye is injected near the inflow, in a band matching the cylinder's own height, so any wake behind it is visible. Open the console for a periodic diagnostic readout (velocity/pressure/dye/divergence min/max, plus whether the pressure solve actually converged).

The correctness test for surface tension, not a demo. A blob of one liquid sits in another with no gravity; the only force in the domain is grid.createSurfaceTension2. At equilibrium the Young–Laplace relation says the pressure inside a 2D blob must exceed the pressure outside by exactly σ/R, so the green readout is the whole point: it compares the measured jump against the closed-form answer. A blob that stays round and a ratio that settles near 1 means the curvature, the sign and the scaling are all right. Add ?sigma= or ?radius= to the URL and the prediction has to track them — a fixed fudge factor cannot follow both.

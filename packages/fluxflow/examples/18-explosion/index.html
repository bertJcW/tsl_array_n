A single, one-shot burst (not the continuous source examples/17-smoke-fire/ uses) -- a large, hot, dense disc near the bottom, plus a brief outward velocity impulse, then nothing: no further injection at all. What happens next is entirely buoyancy and momentum (grid.createGridSmokeSolver2, the same reusable solver as examples/17) acting on that one initial condition. A mushroom-cloud shape, if it appears, is not hand-drawn or scripted -- it is the well-known vortex-ring instability of a large buoyant/momentum-driven blob rising through cooler, denser surrounding fluid (the same mechanism real explosion/rising-thermal simulations rely on), an emergent consequence of buoyancy plus advection, nothing added specifically to produce that shape.

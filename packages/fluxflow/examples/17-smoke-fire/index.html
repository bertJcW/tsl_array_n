A reusable smoke/fire solver (grid.createGridSmokeSolver2, packages/fluxflow/src/grid/grid_smoke_solver2.js) -- a small hot source near the bottom continuously injects density and temperature; buoyancy (density sinks, heat rises, ported from jet/fluid-engine-dev's own GridSmokeSolver2) drives the plume upward through an open-topped domain (an outflow strip along the top wall, so smoke actually leaves rather than piling up against a closed ceiling). "Fire" here is not a separate physics model -- it's the same density+temperature solver, rendered with a temperature-driven color ramp near the hot source instead of plain grayscale. Toggle the checkbox to confirm buoyancy is actually doing the work: with it off, the source should still spread by diffusion/advection alone but stop visibly rising.

grid.createGridTwoPhaseFlipSolver2 in its miscible mode — dye injected into water. The dye is a continuous concentration carried on each particle, not a field advected on the grid, so it transports with no numerical diffusion and only blends as much as you ask it to. dye density is the real physical dial: set it to 1.000 and the dye is a purely passive tracer (uniform density, so the projection reduces exactly to the constant-density one); push it above and the dye sinks and plumes, below and it rises — all through the same variable-density projection a gas bubble uses, just gently. mixing blends each particle toward its surroundings so the dye eventually goes uniform; fade makes it disappear instead. Both are off by default and cost nothing when off. Open the console for a periodic diagnostic readout.
